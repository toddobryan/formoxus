**Asked for on 2026-09-29**, verbatim: *"could you maybe label things you want me
to do with numbers and things I might want to respond to with letters. That
would make it much easier for me to respond. Just avoid bullets altogether, if
you can."*

So, in replies to him:

1. **Numbers** for things he should act on — decisions to make, code to write,
   commands to run, things to check.

(a) **Letters** for things he may want to respond to — findings, options,
reasoning he might push back on, anything offered rather than asked.

**Why:** he answers long replies by label ("1. Do you think…", "2. Eww."), and
an unlabelled bullet list gives him nothing to point at. He had been doing this
by counting my bullets, which is work I created.

**How to apply:** no bullet lists in replies to him — numbered items, lettered
items, tables or prose instead. This is about REPLIES; tables are still fine,
and it does not govern bullets inside a file, a commit message, an issue body or
a memory entry, which have their own readers. Relates to
[[feedback-show-scope-before-writing-code]]: both are about making it cheap for
him to steer.
